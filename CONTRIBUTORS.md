## Contributors

Open a pull request and add yourself — one line, alphabetical by name or handle,
**80 characters or less**:

- **At most one link, to an identity page you own** — your GitHub profile or
  personal site.
- **An optional short, factual note** — an area you worked on or your
  affiliation.

Entries over the limit may be reformatted or trimmed without notice. Your credit
doesn't depend on this line: it's carried by the `Co-authored-by:` trailers on
release commits and your DCO sign-offs.
